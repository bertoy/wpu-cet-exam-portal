Removal examination — {{ course.code }}

Candidates are those whose computed standing falls between {{ course.removal_low|pct }}% and {{ course.removal_high|pct }}%.

How this works

  • Candidacy is decided from the final computed standing, so this page is only meaningful once the term's marks are in.
  • A candidate who passes the removal examination gets exactly 3.00 — the mark is not transmuted.
  • Holding a removal examination adjusts everyone who already passed one grade step higher: 2.50 becomes 2.25, 1.25 becomes 1.00, and so on.
  • None of it takes effect until you finalise the subject, and finalising can be undone.
