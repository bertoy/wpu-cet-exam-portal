How to read this

Difficulty is the share of students who got the item right: 0.85 means 85% did. An item above 0.90 is very easy; below 0.25 it is very hard, and worth checking that the wording and the answer key are correct.

Discrimination compares the top third of the class with the bottom third. Above 0.30 means the item separates those who know the material from those who do not. Near zero means it tells you little. A negative figure means the weaker students did better than the stronger ones, which almost always points to a wrong key or a misleading question. {% if not grouped %} With fewer than six attempts the thirds are too small to be meaningful, so discrimination is left blank. {% endif %}
