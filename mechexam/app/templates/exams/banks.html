Why keep a bank

Questions written straight into an exam disappear with it when the term rolls over. Questions kept in a bank can be tagged by topic and difficulty, pulled into a quiz in a single step, and drawn at random so that no two students in the room sit quite the same paper. Pulling from a bank makes a copy, so editing a question inside an exam never alters the bank.
