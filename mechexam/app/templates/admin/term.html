Behaviour

Answers are saved shortly after a student stops typing, but never more often than this. A student who loses power therefore loses at most this many seconds of work — which is why it cannot be set beyond 120 seconds, however much server time a longer gap would save.

The countdown runs on the student's own device; this only re-checks it against the server and notices an exam you have closed early. Raising it costs nothing but a slightly later reaction to an early close, and it is the single biggest saving on a metered host.

ℹ

For a large class on a limited plan, 45 seconds for saving and 90 for the clock cuts the server time of a two-hour exam by roughly two thirds, and still keeps every student's work within 45 seconds of safe.
