What is and is not moved

The laptop is treated as authoritative for what happened during the examination, and for nothing else. Nothing is ever deleted.

  • A student already on the main portal keeps their name and password. Only a student who is not there at all is created.
  • An attempt already submitted on the main portal is left alone and reported, because an essay may have been marked or a score adjusted there since.
  • A gradebook mark is written only if it is newer than the one here, or if there is none.
  • Final grades are never sent. Finalising a subject is deliberate and belongs where the records are kept.
  • The subject and the examination must already exist here with the same code, term and title. Anything that cannot be matched is reported rather than guessed at.

Sending twice does the same as sending once, so a retry after a dropped connection is safe.
