What it can and cannot do

The assistant writes question sheets. It has no power to save anything: what it produces is handed to the same importer as a spreadsheet uploaded by hand, and the instructor sees every question on the review screen before a single one reaches a bank. A wrong or malformed answer is reported row by row, exactly as a typo in a spreadsheet would be.

  • It cannot read the gradebook, student records or marks.
  • It cannot mark, change or release anything.
  • It cannot write to a bank or an exam without a person pressing Import.
  • Conversations are private to the instructor who held them.
⚠

On a free PythonAnywhere account this will not work. Outbound traffic from a free account is restricted to an allowlist that does not include the API, so the assistant will report that it cannot connect however correct the key is. On a paid account, set HTTPS_PROXY to http://proxy.server:3128 and reload the web app. On Railway or any ordinary host, no proxy is needed.

Questions sent to the API include what the instructor typed and the subject's code and title. Student names and marks are never sent.
